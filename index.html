Longevity Milestone Analysis: Who Wins at What Age? Actuarial Comparison

Because annuities transfer longevity risk, the economic outcome depends on how long the annuitant lives. This table illustrates the black and white financial outcome if life ceases at each respective age milestone.

Age Milestone Years in Payout Annuity Total Value CD/Bond Cash Drawn CD/Bond Ending Principal CD/Bond Total Value Net Winner / Advantage
State 1: Annuity Contract 🔒 Guaranteed Income Floor
  • ✓ Longevity Insurance: Guaranteed level monthly payout of $850/mo cannot be outlived, even if you survive past 100 years old.
  • ✓ Zero Market & Reinvestment Risk: Income payment is fixed and insulated from bond yield drops or economic recessions.
  • ✕ Zero Capital Residual: Once annuitized (life option), the principal is surrendered to the insurer. Beneficiaries receive $0 upon death.
  • ✕ Zero Liquidity: Capital is permanently locked. You cannot withdraw a lump sum for unforeseen medical bills or emergencies.
  • ! Fixed Nominal Purchasing Power: Because payout does not increase by a % a month, inflation steadily erodes purchasing power over 20–30 years.
State 2: CD / Bond Ladder 🏦 Capital Ownership & Liquidity
  • ✓ 100% Capital Ownership: You retain legal title to the money. All residual balance passes directly to your heirs or estate.
  • ✓ Compounding Growth During Deferral: Your initial lump sum grows undisturbed to $160,651 before any withdrawal occurs.
  • ✓ Complete Liquidity: Principal remains accessible at any time (subject only to minimal early CD withdrawal interest adjustments).
  • ✓ Government Backing: CDs are FDIC-insured up to $250k per depositor/institution; Treasury bonds are backed by the full faith and credit of the U.S. Government.
  • ! Depletion Risk: If withdrawals exceed interest earned, principal will eventually deplete if you live beyond the portfolio's exhaustion threshold.
